Empowering Everyone with AI. Everyone is a Creator!

"Will AI replace humans?"

This is the question we've heard most over the past year.

But I prefer to ask: How can AI amplify human capabilities, making creation more efficient and effortless?

I believe AI will not replace humans, but will make every individual more powerful. We can realize better ideas in less time, boost work efficiency, and make life more enjoyable.

From Short Video & Live Streaming to AI: An Unchanging Path of Empowering Creation

A decade ago, I founded LiveMe, a live streaming app and served as an early board member of musical.ly (the precursor to TikTok). Back then, we enabled people to record their lives and express themselves freely using mobile video. The mobile internet lowered the barrier to creation, unleashing unprecedented creative enthusiasm.

Now, we stand at the forefront of the AI wave. Our team has revitalized the classic app PhotoGrid, using AI to make tasks like photo collaging, beautifying, background removal, and style changes accomplishable with a single click. We've also launched KaCha, which magically generates videos from a single photo. AI is reshaping the way we create.

Why Build Ima Studio?

Over these years, we've consistently pursued one mission: empowering creation with technology.

As I started frequently using various AI models myself, I discovered that even industry professionals can easily get overwhelmed by the complexity of tools and the diversity of models. The creative process itself is a complex, multi-step, multimodal workflow, leaving ordinary users at a loss.

I began to wonder: Why not have an intelligent creative assistant? An Agent that functions like a team, automatically understanding creative intent, planning workflows, calling upon the right tools, and accompanying users throughout their creative journey.

This is the origin of Ima Studio (Intelligent Media Agent Studio).

Ima Studio: Your AI Creative Team at Hand

Just give Ima a simple instruction, and it handles the entire process from inspiration to finished product— Copywriting, storyboard design, image generation, video editing, music scoring, sound effects... It's like having a professional creative team, composed of intelligent Agents, ready to respond to your creative needs around the clock.

Whether you want to:

  • Make a fun pet video
  • Create a custom comic book for your child
  • Reshoot a missing video scene
  • Produce a visually stunning brand advertisement
  • Design a logo and poster for a cafe

Ima can handle it all in one place. Creation has never been this simple!

Logo Meaning: A Drop of Inspiration

Ima's logo is inspired by a drop of water, symbolizing the source of inspiration and the freedom of transformation. It also resembles a drop of paint, capable of expanding into vibrant images and content.

It's also a "dialog box"—signifying that simply conversing with Ima brings your ideas to life, freeing you from complex tools and interfaces. This is the revolution brought by AI Agents: a new era of creation unlocked by natural language.

Our Vision

The value of AI lies not in replacement, but in amplifying human creativity.
