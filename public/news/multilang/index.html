Frequently Asked Questions

Answer your concerns to make participation smoother

Q

Who can participate in this "Creator Partnership Program"?

A

As long as you are a creator on one of the platforms: Civitai, Shakker, LibLib, or Seaart, you can join this program. You can apply to join yourself or be invited by others.

Q

Can I invite others? What rewards are there?

A

Of course! For each creator you successfully invite to complete registration, you will get 200 credits, with no level restrictions and unlimited accumulation!

Q

How do I prove I'm a creator on a certain platform?

A

You can submit your homepage link on the original platform at the activity entrance, and we will judge the level based on the homepage information. It is recommended to keep your homepage public for easy verification.

Q

What do I need to do to "successfully join"?

A

Register an ImaStudio account + upload at least 1 work. After completing these two steps, you can claim your rewards.

Q

If I'm both an invitee and want to invite others, can I get double rewards?

A

Of course! You can both receive registration rewards based on your own level and get credits for inviting others. The two reward mechanisms don't conflict - the more active you are, the more you earn!

Q

How long does it take for invitees to receive rewards?

A

Rewards will be credited within 7 working days after you complete the registration review, and you will be notified via site message. If you haven't received them after this time, please contact customer service for assistance.
